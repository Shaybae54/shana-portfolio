How I think

Good design connects the dots.

I start with people, data, and the bigger picture. Whether it's a checkout flow or a loyalty program, I look at how everything connects — and I bring the right people together to make progress. Here's what guides my work:

01

Research before assumptions.

I talk to real users, dig into the data, and understand the full context before jumping to solutions.

02

Systems over screens.

A single feature can impact loyalty, support, and revenue. I design with the whole ecosystem in mind.

03

Collaboration creates clarity.

I run workshops that bring the right people to the table and turn ambiguity into decisions.

04

Experimentation drives progress.

I treat opinions as hypotheses. I prototype, test, and iterate based on evidence, not ego.
